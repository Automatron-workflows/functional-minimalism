Illustrated workflow · fictional dataInspired by Dieter Rams ↗

Start closer.
Spend less fixing.

Why

Repeated redesigns cost time and tokens. Put your design system and functional-minimalism rules into the first brief, then keep the decisions you accept.

What

Functional Minimalism is a local design harness: your system, a clear task, accepted decisions, and checks. Functional minimalism is its philosophy: less effort to understand and act.

How

Connect a design pack. Brief the screen. Build with your coding agent. Render, review, and retain accepted corrections.

Inspired by Dieter Rams’s design principles. Keep useful labels, information, and consequences. Each product keeps its own character.

Explore the source on GitHub ↗

Fewer retries are the goal; time and token savings have not been benchmarked. The film is an illustration. The workshop runs local fixtures and real checks. Live Figma import and an Alps pack are not connected.
