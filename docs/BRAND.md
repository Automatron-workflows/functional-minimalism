# Formthread

**Less to read. More to use.**

Functional minimalism for AI-built interfaces. Your design instinct, carried into every build.

Formthread is Automatron's design harness for AI-built interfaces. It carries a design system, accepted decisions, and review criteria into the next build so the user does not have to repeat the same art direction.

## Philosophy

Functional minimalism is the practical philosophy. It draws on [Dieter Rams’s principles](../references/functional-minimalism.md) while applying our own software review practices. Do not attribute the term to Rams or imply endorsement. Usefulness and comprehension decide what stays. There is no arbitrary word-reduction target.

## Why

A new AI conversation often starts the design conversation over. The team explains its taste again, removes the same filler again, and corrects the same generic patterns. Formthread turns approved judgment into project context that can be reused and checked.

## What

A local, inspectable design workflow with versioned design packs, task-specific briefs, scoped decision memory, and source/rendered-evidence checks. The coding agent does the design work; Formthread supplies continuity and checks. It does not call a model or automatically produce finished screens.

## How

Connect a design pack. Define the task. Generate the brief. Build with the coding agent. Render, inspect, and repair. Keep explicitly accepted corrections for the next screen.

## Name and relationship to Hardwire

Form is the interface people use. Thread is the judgment carried through its creation. The name is pronounced exactly as the two familiar words. Use `Formthread` in prose and `formthread` in the wordmark and CLI.

Hardwire makes application actions explicit for agents. Formthread carries design judgment into human interfaces. Both belong to Automatron, but neither requires the other.

## Visual identity

- Midnight canvas, soft ivory type, restrained warm copper thread. Avoid turning the whole interface into an accent-colored surface.
- A continuous path bends into a compact lowercase f. Use the mark at small sizes and the plain lowercase wordmark for recognition.
- System sans-serif for utility; restrained serif italic for the human phrase in the hero. No remote font dependency.
- The thread provides continuity in motion: a design choice travels into several different screens. Motion must show a relationship, and playback always has pause/replay and reduced-motion support.
- Keep the first view to a wordmark, one headline, one concise category line, and the demonstration. Put fuller why/what/how and setup behind clear controls.
- Art direction belongs to Formthread's own site. Consuming applications keep their own design packs, palettes, density, and character.

## Product language

Use concrete words: design system, task, screen, accepted decision, check, build. Public explanation uses “design memory”; implementation documentation can explain its scoped records. Avoid claiming universal model compatibility, measured usability gains, autonomous design, or a live Figma connection before those exist.
